46Next , we will divide into six working groups again. 
Each group will collect and note any additional functional and non-functional 
capabilities that are expected of CandidateInsight in the future so that this new 
application can be fully integrated into the scenario outlined so far.
Each group goes through the following three steps:
-Identify and determine the categories in which you suspect relevant requirements exist (e.g., 
security).
-Collect and describe concrete requirements for CandidateInsight that you consider relevant.
-For each of the requirements you have identified, name the responsible stakeholder group with 
whom you will confirm the requirement as well as discuss it in more detail.
Collect your results and then present them to the whole class.
