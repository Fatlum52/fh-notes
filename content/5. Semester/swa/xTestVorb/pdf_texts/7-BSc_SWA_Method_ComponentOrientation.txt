7Components make their capabilities available to the outside via dedicated 
interfaces (the providing component exports its interfaces).
Components usethe capabilities of other components via interfaces (the 
consuming component imports interfaces). 
Components bind to interfaces –notto implementations .
In the example below, component CBexports interface S, while CAimports
interface S .

There are a variety of responsibilities 
associated with the Candidate domain concept , 
such as ...
• Registration of new candidates and lifecycle
management of existing candidates 
(CandidateFactory, Candidate, CandidateRepository).
• Management of profile information 
(CandidateProfile).
• Change of status (e.g., “invited,” “evaluated,” 
“rejected”) and logic for status management 
(CandidateStatus).
• Data storage, etc.
The above responsibilities could be combined in 
a CandidateManagement component .
